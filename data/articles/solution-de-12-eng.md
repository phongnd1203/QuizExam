QUESTION 1: PROJECT CHARTER & MEASURABLE OBJECTIVES (QQC) (2.5 Points)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Project Name:
Local Food & Culture Festival "Flavors of Vietnam" Project.

2. Project Purpose & Justifications:
- Main Purpose:
  To promote the cultural heritage and traditional culinary arts of Vietnam's 63 provinces at the central cultural hub of Hanoi.
- Reason 1 (Stimulate Domestic Tourism):
  To stimulate domestic tourism spending during the post-summer shoulder season.
- Reason 2 (Attract International Visitors):
  To attract international tourists to Hanoi and position the capital as an authentic, safe, and culturally rich culinary destination.

3. 4 Project Constraints:
- 1. Scope Constraint:
  + In-Scope:
    * Construction and operation of 200 standard food booths showcasing regional delicacies.
    * Construction of 3 main stages for folk art performances and traditional music.
    * Setup of a traditional craft-making experiential zone.
    * Setup of an entrance arch and interactive photo booth check-in zone for social media engagement.
  + Out-of-Scope:
    * Commercial trade fairs and industrial product exhibitions.
    * Online e-commerce sales using the official festival branding.
- 2. Time Constraint:
  All preparatory work (permits, vendor selection, construction) must be completed strictly within 4 months; the festival operates for exactly 3 consecutive days (Friday to Sunday).
- 3. Cost Constraint:
  Total budget ceiling is fixed at 3,200,000,000 VND (3.2 Billion VND) (2.0B from Hoan Kiem District + 1.2B from sponsorships). Overspending is strictly prohibited.
- 4. Quality Constraint:
  + 100% inter-agency permits approved with full food safety and hygiene compliance.
  + Attract at least 50,000 visitors over the 3-day event.
  + Zero food safety / poisoning incidents.
  + Zero public security incidents.
  + Visitor satisfaction score >= 4.0 / 5.0 in the post-event survey.

4. 3 Measurable Objectives (QQC Model):
- Objective 1 (Quantity):
  Build 200 standard booths, operate 3 stages continuously over 3 days, and attract at least 50,000 attendees.
- Objective 2 (Quality):
  Achieve 0 food poisoning incidents, 0 security issues, and an attendee satisfaction score >= 4.0 / 5.0.
- Objective 3 (Cost & Time):
  Hand over 100% of site infrastructure at least 24 hours prior to opening, with total settled expenses <= 3.2 Billion VND.

2. Risk Management (2 Critical Operational Risks):

Risk 1: Food Safety & Hygiene Incident Risk
- Description & Impact: A vendor utilizes spoiled or uncertified ingredients resulting in food poisoning among visitors -> Violates the "Zero food safety incidents" criterion, damages festival reputation, and risks event suspension by authorities.
- Assessment: Likelihood: Medium | Impact: High
- Strategy: Mitigate
- Mitigation Plan (BEFORE occurrence):
  * Require 100% of vendors to provide certified food origin documentation prior to signing contracts.
  * Provide continuous clean water and dedicated refrigeration/ice containers for perishable goods.
  * Dispatch an inter-agency rapid testing team to inspect food samples daily before opening hours.
- Contingency Plan (AFTER occurrence):
  * Activate the on-site emergency medical tent and dispatch ambulances to transfer patients to the nearest hospital.
  * Immediately seal contaminated food supplies, close the offending booth, and confiscate security deposits.
  * Release a transparent official statement to reassure the public.

Risk 2: Localized Overcrowding & Traffic Congestion around Hoan Kiem Lake
- Description & Impact: Visitor foot traffic exceeds 50,000 during peak hours (Saturday evening), causing severe pedestrian congestion and safety hazards for children and the elderly.
- Assessment: Likelihood: High | Impact: Medium
- Strategy: Mitigate
- Mitigation Plan (BEFORE occurrence):
  * Coordinate with District Police to establish 5 remote parking zones and vehicle exclusion perimeters.
  * Evenly distribute the 200 booths and 3 stages around the lake perimeter to prevent choke points.
- Contingency Plan (AFTER occurrence):
  * Deploy soft crowd barriers to enforce one-way pedestrian circulation along the promenade.
  * Utilize PA systems to guide visitors toward less congested auxiliary stages.
